Humanitarian Architecture Observatory

A living atlas of the spaces where people live, drawn by the people who live in them.

We document the places where people live, from planned refugee camps to self-built settlements, so they can be seen, planned and recognised. Anyone can join: create an account, upload a short video of your space, and Points of Home turns it into a 3D model and a 2D plan.
