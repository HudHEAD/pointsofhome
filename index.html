Humanitarian Observatory of Architecture

Humanitarian crises deserve architectural figuration.

Humanitarian Observatory of Architecture advocates for individual and community agency in planning, legal recognition or self-documentation of spaces denying human rights.

Anyone can join: with your smartphone, start to film to access state-of-the-art 2D plans of your space. All data is encrypted, not retained, and finally yours. When it matters to you, or to get more visibility, share it on the Observatory.
